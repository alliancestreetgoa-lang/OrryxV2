Four parts of the work.

05 Questions

Management consultancy, answered plainly.

What is management consultancy?

Management consultancy is advice to owners and leadership teams on how a business is organised and run. At Orryx it covers operating model and organisation design, process and performance improvement, governance, roles and reporting lines, and change and transformation support.

Is Orryx licensed for management consultancy?

Yes. Management consultancy is activity 7020.00 on the company's business licence number 2538901.01, issued by the Meydan Free Zone, Dubai. The licence was issued on 17 October 2025 and is valid to 16 October 2027.

How does a management consultancy engagement start?

It starts with the owner's real question (growth, control, cash or exit) and the facts behind it. We then examine how the business is run, separate symptoms from causes, and set out the options and a plan in plain language.
