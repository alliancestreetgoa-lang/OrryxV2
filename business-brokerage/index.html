05 Questions

Business brokerage, answered plainly.

What does a business broker do?

A business broker arranges the purchase and sale of businesses. Orryx prepares a business for sale, finds and introduces buyers or sellers, supports the negotiation of terms and coordinates the steps through to completion.

Who is Orryx's business brokerage for?

For sellers who own small and medium sized businesses or professional practices, and for buyers: entrepreneurs and investors looking to acquire an established business.

Does Orryx broker real estate?

No. The licensed activity (7490.02, business licence 2538901.01, Meydan Free Zone, Dubai) covers arranging the purchase and sale of small and medium sized businesses, including professional practices. It does not include real estate brokerage.
