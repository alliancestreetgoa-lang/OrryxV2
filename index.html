06 Questions

Orryx, answered plainly.

What does Orryx Commodities do?

Orryx Commodities L.L.C-FZ is a Dubai consultancy. It is licensed for four activities: management consultancy, strategy advisory, financial consultancy, and the brokerage of small and medium sized businesses.

Does Orryx Commodities trade commodities?

Commodity trading is not among the activities on the company's current business licence. Orryx is an advisory and business brokerage firm: it advises owners and leadership teams, and arranges the purchase and sale of businesses.

Is Orryx Commodities a licensed company?

Yes. Orryx Commodities L.L.C-FZ is a limited liability company formed in the Meydan Free Zone, Dubai, under business licence number 2538901.01. The licence was issued on 17 October 2025 and is valid to 16 October 2027.

Does Orryx broker real estate?

No. The brokerage activity covers arranging the purchase and sale of small and medium sized businesses, including professional practices. It does not include real estate brokerage.

Where is Orryx Commodities based, and who manages it?

The registered address is Meydan Grandstand, 6th floor, Meydan Road, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai, U.A.E. The manager named on the business licence is Stallone Dominic Shaikh.

How does an engagement with Orryx work?

In five steps: Listen, Diagnose, Design, Deliver and, where buying or selling a business is the right move, Transact. It starts with the owner's real question: growth, control, cash or exit.
