01 What it covers

Four parts of the work.

01

Growth and market-entry planning

Where growth can come from (new customers, new services or new markets) and what entering each one would take.

02

Business plans and strategic roadmaps

A written plan that sets out the goal, the steps, the order they happen in and who owns each one.

03

Competitive positioning

Where the business stands against the alternatives a customer has, and what it should be known for.

04

Board and owner decision support

The options, the trade-offs and a recommendation, set out in plain language before a major decision is made.

02 When it fits

Signs it is time to talk.

  • Growth has slowed and the next step is not obvious.
  • A new market or service is on the table.
  • The owners want one agreed plan, not several opinions.
  • A major decision needs a clear, written case.

05 Questions

Strategy advisory, answered plainly.

What is strategy advisory?

Strategy advisory helps owners and boards decide where the business goes next and in what order. At Orryx it covers growth and market-entry planning, business plans and strategic roadmaps, competitive positioning, and board and owner decision support.

Is Orryx licensed for strategy advisory?

Yes. Strategy advisory services is activity 7020.11 on the company's business licence number 2538901.01, issued by the Meydan Free Zone, Dubai. The licence was issued on 17 October 2025 and is valid to 16 October 2027.

What do I get at the end of a strategy engagement?

The options, a recommended strategy and the plan to reach it, written in plain language. Where the client wants it, Orryx stays alongside management while the plan is carried out.
